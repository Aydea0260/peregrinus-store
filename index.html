Authentic flavors. Practical solutions.

Our fruit pulps in pouches offer unmatched versatility for preparing juices, aguas frescas and smoothies — just place the pouch in the blender, add water or milk, and serve fresh, natural fruit drinks, with or without sugar. Ideal for restaurants and any establishment.

ProductFruit pulp pouches
FlavorsPassionfruit, soursop, guava, mango, watermelon, tangerine, blackberry, tamarind, pineapple, Jamaica, horchata, melon, mango biche, cherry lemonade, piña colada and more
StorageKeep frozen
Shelf life18 months frozen
PackagingBoxes with 10 bags, each bag containing 10 pouches of 3.5 oz

Guacamole

Real ingredients, authentic flavor — made with authentic Hass avocados and a blend of fresh ingredients. Thaw in the refrigerator (40°F), stir well, and serve as is or customize.

Ingredients

Hass avocado, onion, red bell pepper, lemon juice, jalapeño pepper, salt, antioxidant (ascorbic acid), stabilizer (xanthan gum), garlic powder.

Uses

Dips, tacos, burgers, salads, sandwiches & wraps, nachos, burrito bowls and foodservice menu items.

  • Shelf life: 1 year frozen (365 days) · Storage 0°F (−18°C)
  • Country of origin: Colombia
  • Gluten Free · Cholesterol Free · Kosher
